    How to verify a round

    1. Before the roundYou see the server seed hash. You don't know the seed yet, but the hash pins it down.
    2. During the roundThe result is HMAC-SHA256 of the server seed, your client seed and the round number (nonce).
    3. After rotating the seedYou get the previous server seed and can check its hash and every round's result in the verifier.
